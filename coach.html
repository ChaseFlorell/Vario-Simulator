Catching a thermal, by ear

Put headphones on. This trainer teaches one skill: hearing lift on a paragliding vario, turning into it, and staying in the strongest part. The sound is the instrument — the map beside it is the instructor's view, and in the air you would not have it.

Rising pitch and faster beeps mean you are going up. A low growl means you are sinking out and should leave. Silence is the deadband either side of zero.

SliderWeight shift. Slide left or right to bank, and hold it there to stay in the turn — let go and it eases back to level. ← →The same bank from the keyboard. These hold where you leave them, so use the keys to settle into a long climb. XWings level. ↑ ↓Speed bar on / off. TThermal sniffer — show what the instrument infers about the core. Start with Exercise 1: one steady thermal, straight ahead, no wind, and the air revealed so you can watch the sound and the lift line up.
