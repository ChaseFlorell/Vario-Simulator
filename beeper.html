Vario Beeper

Turn your volume up. Drag the slider and hear exactly what a paragliding vario does at every climb rate — faster, higher beeps going up; a low growl going down.

It starts at zero, which is silent. That band of quiet either side of zero is the deadband, and it is why a vario says nothing when you are just gliding.
